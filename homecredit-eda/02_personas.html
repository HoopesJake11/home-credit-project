The two personas on the slides are based on real, anonymized holdout applicants, scored by the final model. The names are made up. Each applicant’s prediction is split into contributions (SHAP values) that show which parts of the application pushed the risk up or down.

Decision rule used for the personas: the riskiest 10% of applicants by model score are denied; everyone else is approved. Change deny_share to try another rule.

deny_share <- 0.10
cutoff <- quantile(holdout_prob, 1 - deny_share)

ho <- holdout_data |>
  mutate(row_id = row_number(),
         prob = holdout_prob,
         risk_percentile = percent_rank(prob),
         decision = if_else(prob >= cutoff, "Deny", "Approve"))

tibble(`Deny if predicted risk is at least` = pct(cutoff),

Candidate applicants

Approved candidate: no credit bureau record (the borrower a traditional bank turns away), repaid, in the safest half of applicants, has at least two outside scores, aged 22 to 35, currently employed.

Denied candidate: in the riskiest 10%, actually had payment difficulties, aged 22 to 45, and does have a credit bureau record (so the denial is not about missing history).

Why the model decided what it did

ex <- explain_applicants(final_internal_xgb, personas)
stopifnot(max(abs(ex$prob - personas$prob)) < 1e-4)  # contributions add up to the score

contrib <- ex$long |>
  mutate(persona = personas$persona[row], theme = theme_of(variable))

by_theme <- contrib |>
  group_by(persona, theme) |>
  summarise(contribution = sum(contribution), .groups = "drop") |>
  filter(abs(contribution) > 0.005) |>
  arrange(persona, desc(abs(contribution)))

by_feature <- contrib |>
  filter(contribution != 0) |>
  group_by(persona) |>
  slice_max(abs(contribution), n = 8) |>
  ungroup() |>
  mutate(direction = if_else(contribution > 0, "raises risk", "lowers risk"))

Summary for the slides

avg_risk <- mean(ho$prob)
fmt_profile <- function(r) {
  c(sprintf("- Predicted risk: %s (average applicant %s); riskier than %s of applicants; decision: %s",
            pct(r$prob), pct(avg_risk), pct(r$risk_percentile), r$decision),
    sprintf("- Actual outcome: %s", if_else(r$TARGET == 1, "had payment difficulties", "repaid")),
    sprintf("- Age %d, %s, %s; employed %s years; %s",
            round(r$age_years), r$CODE_GENDER, r$NAME_FAMILY_STATUS,
            round(r$years_employed, 1), r$NAME_INCOME_TYPE),
    sprintf("- Occupation: %s; education: %s; housing: %s; children: %s",
            r$OCCUPATION_TYPE, r$NAME_EDUCATION_TYPE, r$NAME_HOUSING_TYPE, r$CNT_CHILDREN),
    sprintf("- Loan %s (%s) for goods priced %s, loan-to-value %.2f; income %s",
            num(r$AMT_CREDIT), r$NAME_CONTRACT_TYPE, num(r$AMT_GOODS_PRICE), r$ltv,
            num(r$AMT_INCOME_TOTAL)),
    sprintf("- Outside score average %.2f from %d score(s); credit bureau record: %s; blank fields: %d",
            r$ext_source_mean, as.integer(r$ext_source_count),
            if_else(r$has_bureau_record == 1, "yes", "no"), as.integer(r$fields_missing)))
}

  r <- personas[i, ]

             fmt_profile(r),

             "- Biggest factors raising risk:",
             paste0("  - ", raise$theme, " (", round(raise$contribution, 2), ")"),
             "")
}
